Record Integrity
==============================================================================
  Every log record carries an 8-byte integrity value covering the record itself. The
  driver recomputes it before replaying the record; forefst recomputes it the same way.
  A single flipped bit or byte always changes it, but it is the XOR of the record's 32-bit
  words: any reordering of those words, or changes whose XOR cancels, keep the value. It
  is an integrity check, not a signature, and not proof of authenticity.
